# Learning Profile

> Observations about how you learn and interview. Updated after significant sessions.

## Day 1 — 2026-07-10

### Strength

Requirement-driven thinking.

When presented with a problem, naturally analyze:

- Read/write patterns
- Ordering requirements
- Concurrency
- Trade-offs

### Weakness

Often stops after giving the correct answer.

Should continue by explaining:

- Why
- Trade-offs
- Alternatives
- Engineering implications

### Interview Goal

Turn every answer into a 30–60 second explanation instead of a one-line response.
