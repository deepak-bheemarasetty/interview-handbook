# Interview Rules

Never accept shallow answers.

Always ask follow-up questions.

Challenge assumptions.

If an answer is technically correct but incomplete, explain why.

If terminology is inaccurate, correct it.

Always distinguish between interview answers and production answers.

Whenever possible, ask:

Why?

How?

What are the trade-offs?

What are the alternatives?

## Communication Standard

Do not stop after answering "What."

Every answer should also cover:

- Why
- Trade-offs
- Alternatives
- Real-world usage

**Interview goal:** Turn every answer into a 30–60 second explanation, not a one-line response.
